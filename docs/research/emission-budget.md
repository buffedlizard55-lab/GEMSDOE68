# Fixed H68-M1 emission budget

H68-M1 preregistered **38,000 dots**, nonmaximum suppression radius 5 px and a 2-pixel exclusion from the mapped catalogue, before fitting or inspecting the four outer holdouts. Every stage is compared at this *same* budget. This is a design choice, **not an inferred private-score optimum**. The official objective is documented at [DrivenData](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/); the actual local comparison is on the [results page](../results.html). Earlier budget analysis used 40,000 dots and was archived unchanged as `evidence/archives/2026-10-12/emission-budget-old.md`, not reused for H68-M1.
