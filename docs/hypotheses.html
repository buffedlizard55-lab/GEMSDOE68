Validation status, checked 2026-10-20. H68-1 has historical model-level HOLDOUT-DTI evidence in

evidence/ac68_seed20.json (evaluator gems57-pooled-hide-v2, 11,318 withheld positives); a paired

95% CI was not computed, and that evidence is not hash-linked to the latest TIFF. All-pixel

stage-2 vs stage-1 clears the >2-SE test, but the >1 km stratum does not (t=1.97); the candidate is

negative and the final-raster overlap rule fires. H68-3 inputs are present in core data; H68-4 GeoDAWN

radiometric inputs were restored from the hash-pinned mirror and still need an official-layer audit;

neither has been experimentally validated here. H68-2 needs USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM coverage; H68-5 needs

USGS ComCat/rupture products. Their live retrieval was not tested in this turn, so availability is

not confirmed. These are research backlog proposals, not authorized changes to the auto-context lane.

Named non-fault processes that could mimic each signature (for the run card): H68-1 — any

linear feature the stage-1 model already likes (roads, canals, shoreline terraces, lithologic

contacts) is reinforced by continuity; H68-2 — fluvial terrace risers and pediment edges;

H68-3 — intrusive contacts and dike swarms; H68-4 — lithologic K contrasts in volcanic flows and

playa evaporites; H68-5 — none (direct observation), but positional error of the rupture map.
