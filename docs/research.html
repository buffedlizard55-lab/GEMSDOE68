Problem: the prize target is fault pixels relevant to geothermal exploration, not a training set of geothermal vents. The competition masks its private new expert fault lines. Owner mirrors restore inputs under pinned hashes but cannot replace authenticated organizer downloads. This site makes no statement about an undisclosed 2026 final label set.

Why the geometry matters: the official kernel gives a predicted dot partial credit only within 300 m of a truth pixel. The distance-weighted Tversky index is TP / (TP + 0.2·FP + 0.8·FN), with TP/FP/FN derived from bidirectional nearest-neighbour kernel coverage, not simply a pixelwise classification rate. An elongated 1-pixel line can cover more unique truth than a thick binary mask, but excessive lines spend FP mass. This is why H33-style pruning is plausible—not proof it scored 0.2778 or that this model will improve it.

Physics: GeoDAWN airborne magnetics and radiometrics and reprocessed LiDAR scarps can reveal structural discontinuities; a magnetic edge alone may be a lithologic contact, survey boundary or human infrastructure. Directional offsets and smooths encode this uncertainty; blocked, buffered withheld faults test extrapolation rather than memorizing the labelled catalogue. Read the mass/pruning derivation.
