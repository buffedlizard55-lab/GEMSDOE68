Competition and challenge documents

SourceWhat it fixed
DrivenData competition page 967 — Geothermal Evidence of Machine-learning Discoverable Structures the challenge's definition of the target (geothermal-relevant faults at 100 m), the ≥ 1 km length rule for what counts as a reportable structure, and the fact that the scoring truth is login-walled
DrivenData competition page 968 the submission format: a GeoTiFF of the same grid as target.tif, values in [0, 1], with a free-text note of at most 140 characters
Newberry / NASA Frontier Development Lab competition rules PDF the reporting window (structures must be at least 1 km long), the eligibility rule that human interpretation may not be substituted for the model, and the requirement that everything be reproducible from public data

Geological and geophysical literature

SourceWhat it contributed
USGS GEODawn aerogeophysical survey (doi:10.5066/P93LGLVQ) — the gda_* radiometric / magnetic bands the provenance of the 8 GeoDAWN channels: K, Th, U, TC, TMI, the horizontal gradient of TMI, and the isostatic residual gravity. Also the contractor's K/Th/U ratio convention, which is what the derived ThK, UK and UTh bands use
INGENIOUS fault database (Newberry Volcano, Oregon) the structure of the mapped fault catalogue that supplies both the training positives and the "already known" prior — in particular that mapped faults are recorded as connected strands with junctions, which is what the catalogue-context features must respect
OpenEI Geothermal Data Repository entry 1391 (Newberry) the survey metadata for the airborne EM and magnetics used to interpret the conductivity and magnetic channels
Tversky index (geometric set similarity) the reason the local gate is a spatial DTI with α = 0.2 and not the Tversky index: the gate measures distance-weighted hit mass inside a 300 m triangular kernel, which rewards localisation rather than set overlap

Method literature

SourceWhat it contributed
Tu, Z. "Auto-context and its application to high-level vision tasks", CVPR 2008 (doi:10.1109/CVPR.2008.4587436) and the journal version in IEEE TPAMI the stacked-refinement recipe this session implements: train a per-pixel model, use its probability map as an input to a second model, and repeat. The paper's two cautions — that the context features must be estimated with the target label masked to avoid self-reinforcement, and that each refinement stage needs its own out-of-fold probabilities — are both implemented here
spatial cross-validation / blocked cross-validation practice why the four outer folds are quadrants of the footprint with a 600 m buffer between the inner blocks, rather than random splits: a random split lets a cell's neighbours leak its label and reports an AUC that no future prediction can reproduce
structure tensor / orientation-estimation practice (2-D smoothed gradient outer product) how the local fault orientation is estimated from the detrended-elevation gradient so that the auto-context sampling offsets follow the structure rather than the raster axes
