Method: Auto-Context Stacked Refinement

Based on Tu et al., CVPR 2008 (doi:10.1109/CVPR.2008.4587436). Stage 1 produces per-pixel fault probability maps from 19 GeoDAWN feature bands. Stage 2 learns spatial regularities — continuity along strike, parallelism of adjacent structures, en échelon spacing — that a per-pixel classifier misses.

Stage 2: Auto-Context Spatial Refinement

Input to Stage 2 from Stage 1 ensemble:

  • Multi-scale Gaussian smooths (σ = 1, 2, 4, 8)
  • Gradient magnitude and Laplacian of ensemble
  • Offset samples at ±1, ±3, ±5, ±10 px along 4 directions (horizontal, vertical, 2 diagonals)
  • Maximum offset (ridge strength) and mean offset (continuity)
  • Orientation coherence via structure tensor of ensemble

Why this catches faults missing from the catalogue: The catalogue is dominated by faults mappable in a single dataset. Requiring multi-physics agreement on orientation selects structures an expert accepts only when several fields agree — precisely the class of subtle fault that gets missed in single-dataset mapping.
