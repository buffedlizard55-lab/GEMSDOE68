What this is

A two-stage classifier for the GEMS fault-discovery grid (3730 × 3292 px, EPSG:32611, 100 m). Stage 1 is a per-pixel LightGBM on 47 geophysical channels; stage 2 is auto-context (Tu, CVPR 2008): a second LightGBM trained on stage 1's out-of-fold probability map sampled at ±1–15 px along and across the local strike, plus multi-scale anisotropic smooths, the raw gradient/tilt/scarp channels, and catalogue-context features (distance, orientation and spacing to the nearest mapped fault, with the target block's labels masked). Stage 2 exists to learn what a per-pixel model cannot: continuity along strike, parallelism between splays, en échelon spacing.
