4 · Why this could beat 0.2778 — and what 0.2778 is

The 0.2778 figure for h33-h33-2-b2 is owner-reported: the GEMSDOE32 page the file is downloaded from calls the same bytes UNSCORED with a projected 0.2747, and this repo holds no organiser receipt for it. The ladder behind it (121 k px → 37,654 dots; deleting the 100–200 m catalogue halo) won by mass discipline: every emitted pixel must clear a marginal-credit bar of 0.2·DTI. After pruning, the only remaining lever is ranking, and auto-context (continuity, parallelism, en-échelon spacing learned from the stage-1 probability map) is the principled attack on it. This session tested removing catalogue-context entirely — it failed (catalogue context is load-bearing even >1 km) — and the current candidate instead adds lidar-scarp and radiometric evidence to stage 1. Hypothesis as preregistered: AC68-N2: enriching stage 1 with fold-invariant external channels (2 m lidar scarp aggregates + coverage, GeoDAWN radiometric/extension percentile ranks) improves first-stage ranking enough that unchanged auto-context stages beat the previous holdout best at matched mass.
