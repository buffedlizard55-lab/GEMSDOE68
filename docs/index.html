1. PhD-Level Scientific Analysis: Why h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros Scored 0.2778 & How We Score Higher

Across the GEMSDOE family (registry/score_ledger.csv), the highest organizer-confirmed score is h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros (ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED 0.2778, built in GEMSDOE32). Understanding why that raster achieved 0.2778—and why both dense soft-probability rasters and un-spaced top-N rasters scored lower—follows directly from the algebra of the Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (DTI) with α = 0.2, β = 0.8, and 300 m (R = 3 px) triangular kernel k(d) = max(1 - d/3, 0):

Why h33-h33-2-b2 plateaued at 0.2778 and how Auto-Context Stacked Refinement beats it: Because every rung of the h33-2 ladder only deleted dots from a fixed per-pixel surface, its ranking of pixels at d > 2 px was still produced by a per-pixel classifier that cannot distinguish an isolated noisy topographic step (fluvial terrace, shoreline, road cut) from a multi-kilometer tectonic fault strand, nor can it resolve 1–5 m Holocene scarps washed out by the 100 m competition DEM. Our Experiment 3 Auto-Context Stacked Refinement (Tu, CVPR 2008 / TPAMI 2010) overcomes both limitations by: (1) adding 2 m USGS 3DEP LiDAR fault-scarp channels (scarp_upface_max, scarp_antislope_excess, scarp_crest_toe_dipole) and 1.5 km high-pass GeoDAWN radiometric & upward-continued magnetic dipole residuals to Stage 1 while removing the 5 regional seismicity/geodesy bands that caused 2D regional blobs (IR-68-BLOB-01); and (2) training Stage 2 and Stage 3 with leak-free whole-component hide-draw labels on ±1–15 px along/across-strike probability offsets, multi-scale anisotropic smooths, 1D curvilinear ridge-vs-blob contrast (p_aniso_contrast_6, p_ridge_convexity_3, p_along_bridge), and along-strike fault-tip continuation (cat_along_tip_drop).
