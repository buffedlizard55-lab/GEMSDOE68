3. Spatial Morphology & Resolution of IR-68-BLOB-01

What changed from Experiment 2 to Experiment 3: In Experiment 2, Stage 1 relied on smooth basin-wide lookup fields (det_elev, ieq_n100a15, deq_n100a15, geod_2ndinv, cond_surf) and Stage 2/3 relied on an isotropic σ = 10 px (1 km) smooth (p_sm10), producing 2D area-fill blobs and pulling Stage-3 dot quota out of the > 1 km far-field (NE far Δ = -0.0034, t_far = 1.51 < 2.0). Experiment 3 replaces Stage 1 with 37 purely local physical + 2 m LiDAR scarp + H68-2 across-strike footwall asymmetry (elev_asym) + H68-3 basement gradient (db_grad) + H68-4 high-pass GeoDAWN channels, replaces p_sm10 with anisotropic along/across-strike smooths (p_along_short, p_aniso, p_ridge, ori_coh) and directional 1.5/3.0 km fault-tip relays (cat_relay_15, cat_relay_30), and applies damped rank-probability sequential refinement (α2 = 0.22, α3 = 0.35). Only 20.15 % of Experiment 3's dots overlap within 3 px of Experiment 2's dots.
