## 1. Mathematical & Geological Deconstruction of the `0.2778` Champion (`GEMSDOE32`) vs `0.2750` (`GEMSDOE36`)

### 1.1 Exact Competition Objective (`DTI`) and Bang-Bang Submodularity

For a binary ground-truth hidden fault trace mask $G \subset \Omega$ ($T = |G|$ pixels on the 100 m UTM Zone 11N `EPSG:32611` grid) and a submitted field $y \in [0, 1]^\Omega$ ($P = \sum_{x \in \Omega} y(x)$), the official **Distance-Tolerant Intersection (`DTI`)** metric (NLR/OSTI Report `96647`, `alpha = 0.2`, `beta = 0.8`, linear buffer $R = 300\text{ m} = 3\text{ px}$) is:

$$s_t(x) = \max_{g \in G} y(x)\,\max\!\left(0,\, 1 - \frac{\|x - g\|_2}{3}\right), \qquad s_p(x) = y(x)\,\max_{g \in G}\max\!\left(0,\, 1 - \frac{\|x - g\|_2}{3}\right)$$

$$\mathrm{recall}(y, G) = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{g \in G} \max_{x \in \Omega} y(x)\,K(x, g), \qquad \mathrm{prec}(y, G) = \frac{1}{P}\sum_{x \in \Omega} s_p(x)$$

$$\mathrm{DTI}(y, G) = \left(\frac{0.2}{\mathrm{recall}(y, G)} + \frac{0.8}{\mathrm{prec}(y, G)}\right)^{-1} = \frac{1}{\frac{0.2\,T}{\mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{gt}}} + \frac{0.8\,P}{\mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{pred}}}}.$$

Three structural consequences explain the entire progression of the `GEMSDOE` fleet:

1. **Asymmetric `4 : 1` Precision Weighting ($\beta / \alpha = 0.8 / 0.2 = 4$):** Around the fleet champion operating point ($\mathrm{recall} \approx 0.250$, $\mathrm{prec} \approx 0.395 \implies \mathrm{DTI} = (0.2/0.250 + 0.8/0.395)^{-1} = (0.800 + 2.025)^{-1} = 0.354$ ideal / `0.2778` live `OWNER-REPORT`), each false-positive dot added to $P$ degrades inverse-precision $4\times$ faster than an equivalent fraction of missed fault pixels degrades inverse-recall.
2. **Exact Bang-Bang `{0, 1}` Optimality (`300 m` Submodular Coverage):** For any candidate pixel $x$ at posterior fault probability $p(x) = \mathbb{P}(\exists g \in G : K(x, g) > 0 \mid \mathcal{F})$ and uncovered kernel gain $\Delta R(x \mid S)$ relative to already-emitted dots $S$, the first-order Fréchet derivative $\partial \mathrm{DTI} / \partial y(x)$ is linear in $y(x)$ at any fixed operating ratio $(\mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{gt}}/T, \mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{pred}}/P)$. Consequently, interior fractional values $y(x) \in (0, 1)$ strictly dilute precision whenever neighboring pixels already saturate $\max_{x \in B_3(g)} y(x) K(x, g)$: the global maximizer on the hypercube $[0, 1]^\Omega$ concentrates mass onto discrete **Bang-Bang `{0.0, 1.0}` points spaced by the `300 m` kernel radius** along fault strike.
   - *Direct holdout verification:* Taking the identical pre-scored continuous surface `scored_h19_5.tif` at fixed `38,000`-dot budget, replacing naive `5×5` box NMS with our exact 300 m submodular greedy selector (`emit_submodular_300m`) immediately nearly doubles `HOLDOUT-DTI` from **`0.0481` to `0.0880` (`1.83×`)** on `ALL` and from **`0.0444` to `0.0713` (`1.61×`)** on `FAR (> 1 km)` — with zero model retraining!

### 3.1 Zero-Leakage Nested Spatial Cross-Validation
- **Outer Holdout (`seed=20`, untouched by feature engineering, model training, or budget tuning):** 4 spatial quadrants across Nevada (`NW, NE, SW, SE`), each eroded by `2.6 km` (`5.2 km` minimum Euclidean core-to-core separation, exceeding maximum spatial stencil radius by `> 3×`), withholding `20%` of whole connected fault components (`11,321` `ALL` positive pixels; `5,150` `FAR > 1.0 km` positive pixels) with a `3 px` (`300 m`) visibility collar masked around withheld traces.
- **Inner Hide-World (`inner_worlds[0]`, strictly inside outer `vis_outer`):** Masks `40%` of remaining `vis_outer` connected fault components inside each training block with a `3 px` visibility collar so Stage-2 and Stage-3 auto-context classifiers learn genuine **gap-bridging and off-catalogue discovery** rather than collapsing onto `cat_dist == 0` (`IR-68-LEAK-01`). - **Pre-Model Holdout Leakage Canary (`evidence/s68_leakage_canary.json`):** Every single one of the `59` channels in `X_fp59.npy` was tested alone against withheld holdout faults before fitting a single tree. **59 / 59 channels passed (`max symmetric AUC = 0.6634 << 0.90` ceiling; `0` flagged channels).**
